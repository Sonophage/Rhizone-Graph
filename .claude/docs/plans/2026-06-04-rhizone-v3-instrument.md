# Rhizone v3 — from map to instrument (daily driver)

Goal: make Rhizone something used *daily*, chosen over the native graph or flat notes, because it
**proposes something you didn't know and lets you act in place**. Map → oracle + workbench. Built on
the v2 bones; no rewrite. In-world Bureau/Control voice throughout.

Sequenced A → C → B.

## Pillar A — The Oracle (the daily hook)
The ambient state stops being a silent necklace; on open it hands you a **reading**.
- **Today's door** *(this build)* — a date-seeded **ripe coincidence to forge**: two notes joined ONLY
  by a rare (df=2) facet, not yet connected — "build the stair?" Stable per day (ritual), "another"
  cycles the pool. Actions inline: forge · open A · open B · another.
  - Computed in-view from the cached facet graph + `_links`: facet nodes with `df===2` whose two
    citing notes aren't already linked; phantom df=2 included (two notes gesturing at one unwritten
    thing). No new engine.
- **Three offerings** *(later in A)* — coincidence + a neglected note + an overdue unwritten ghost.
- **"the vault notices…"** *(later in A)* — one Bureau-voice line narrating what surfaced.

## Pillar C — Signal (the graph reads as a reading)
- **Size = load-bearing** — node radius ∝ vault-influence (reuse Reticular's measure).
- **Glow = life** — recently-touched warm, long-neglected cool (frontmatter dates / mtime).
- **Depletion meter** — "N coincidences remain unforged," ticks down as you build stairs.
- **"via {rare facet}" everywhere** — chord/tie hover tooltip + a line under the keystone name.
- **Named constellations** — clusters auto-named by their rarest shared facet.

## Pillar B — Atmosphere (classier, more magical)
- **Ignition on open** — vault wakes from the centre outward (console boot), not a geometric scatter.
- **Summon as a gravitational event** — eased in-fall, centre gravity-well shimmer, breathing glow.
- **Instrument frame** — Etz Chaim as a divination core; thin HUD; one phosphor accent; generous black;
  a faint living idle (drift/twinkle on the rarest nodes).
